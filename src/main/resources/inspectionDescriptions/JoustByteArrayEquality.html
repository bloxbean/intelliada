Reports byte[] compared with == or !=, with byte[].equals, or with Objects.equals on a byte[] operand, inside a class annotated @StateContract.

The same method runs on the JVM and on chain. On the JVM these compare references; on chain they compare bytes, so the two runs can disagree. JuLC rejects them itself (JULC0086 for ==/!=, JULC0087 for equals); this inspection is earlier feedback.

The quick fix rewrites the comparison to ByteStringLib.equals(a, b) and imports org.julclang.stdlib.lib.ByteStringLib.

Advice only: JOUST's build stays the authority (IntelliAda ADR-004 D10).
