Reports IntervalLib.finiteUpperBound and IntervalLib.finiteLowerBound compared without a Finite check, inside a class annotated @StateContract.

Both return -1 for an infinite bound, so comparing the result without first checking that the bound is IntervalBoundType.Finite can accept an unbounded validity interval.

This is a heuristic. A call is reported when its value is compared (the receiver or argument of compareTo/equals, or an operand of a relational or equality operator, directly or through a local variable it is stored in) and no compared use is guarded by a check of that bound: a test against org.julclang.ledger.IntervalBoundType.Finite (instanceof, a case pattern, a cast, or a record pattern) of i.to().boundType() for finiteUpperBound(i), or of i.from().boundType() for finiteLowerBound(i). A check guards a use when it comes first in an enclosing block (not inside a branch of an earlier statement), or sits in the condition of an enclosing if, loop or ?:, in an earlier operand of &&/||, or in a case label of an enclosing switch. Checking the lower bound does not silence the upper one.

Advice only: JOUST's build stays the authority (IntelliAda ADR-004 D10).
